Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

These terms apply to this website and to the consultations offered through it by Alexander Lebedev, MD, PhD (“I”), under the name Dynamic Equilibrium. By sending a request or booking a consultation you accept them.

1. What the service is

Individual consultations and ongoing support that help you understand your situation, identify priorities and make a plan, in three formats: a single consultation (60 minutes), monthly support (three sessions per month) and a ten-session package. Sessions are held online.

2. What it is not

This is consultative and educational support by a physician. It is not a medical service and does not replace the diagnosis, treatment or prescribing of your treating doctors, or emergency care. Impressions and working hypotheses shared in a consultation are for orientation; they are not a formal diagnosis or a treatment decision. I do not change treatment prescribed by other doctors, I do not provide psychotherapy through this service, and I am not acting as your treating physician. Information on this website is general and is not medical advice.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate and complete information, including every medicine and supplement you take.
  • Continue to see your own doctors, and do not start, stop or change prescribed treatment because of a consultation without speaking to the prescriber.
  • Seek medical care promptly if symptoms are new, severe or worrying.

4. Fees and payment

Fees are in euros: single consultation €400 (60 minutes); monthly support €1,000 (three sessions per month); ten-session package €3,000. Laboratory tests, if any, are arranged through you or your own doctor and are not included. Scheduling, rescheduling and payment details are agreed with you in writing before the first paid session. The website does not take payments.

5. Confidentiality

6. Emergencies

This is not an emergency service and I am not available for urgent situations. If you are in danger or may harm yourself, contact local emergency services at once (112 in the EU, 911 in the US) or a crisis line in your country.

7. No guarantees

I work carefully and in line with current evidence, but I cannot guarantee any particular result. Outcomes depend on many factors, including your circumstances and what you decide to do.

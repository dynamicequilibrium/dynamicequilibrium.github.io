Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Dynamic Equilibrium is the private consulting practice of Alexander Lebedev, MD, PhD (“I”). It is run independently of the other organisations I work with, and it does not use data from them. This policy explains what personal data I handle on this website and in consultations, and why.

1. What I collect

  • Requests. Name, e-mail, preferred language and contact channel, and what you write in the contact form.
  • Consultations. What you tell me, including information about your health, and any records or results you share.
  • Arrangements. Scheduling and payment details.
  • Self-reflection tools. Answers you submit through the questionnaires on this site and, if you enter it, your e-mail address.
  • Technical data. Pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and similar analytics data.

2. Why I use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange a consultation.
  • To provide the consultation and follow-up you ask for. Health information is used on the basis of your explicit consent, which you may withdraw.
  • To meet legal obligations.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it.

3. Services that process data for me

  • Formspree receives the contact form and passes it to me.
  • Google Analytics provides website statistics. Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Google Forms receives the answers submitted through the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 questionnaires.
  • Messaging apps (Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal) and e-mail, as you choose, for communication between sessions.
  • Video-call, scheduling and payment tools, used to hold sessions and arrange them.
  • Cloud storage and backup for records, with encrypted backups.
  • Note-taking and AI tools. With your agreement a session may be recorded and transcribed for my notes. I may use AI-based tools to help organise notes and prepare written materials; I aim to remove names and direct identifiers before material is processed in this way.

Some of these providers process data outside your country or the European Economic Area, on the safeguards they offer under their own terms.

4. Recordings

I record or transcribe a session only if you agree at the start, and you may decline or ask me to stop at any time.

5. How long I keep data

I keep consultation records for as long as they are useful for supporting you and as the law requires, and then delete them. You may ask me to delete your data earlier, unless the law requires me to keep it.

6. Security

I limit access to records and keep backups encrypted. Ordinary e-mail and messaging are not perfectly secure channels; if you prefer, say so and we will use another one, and please avoid sending highly sensitive details by ordinary e-mail.

7. Cookies and similar storage

The site uses Google Analytics cookies for statistics, and your browser’s local storage to remember your light/dark theme choice. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them.

8. Your rights

Where the GDPR or similar law applies to you, you may ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and you may object to certain processing and withdraw consent. To do so, use the contact form. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Age

These services are for adults. I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

I may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
